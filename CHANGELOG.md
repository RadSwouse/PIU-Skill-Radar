# Changelog

## 2026-11-06

- **New detection formula for Side: added 233 songs** (378 charts: 213 Singles, 165 Doubles).
  On Doubles you often turn sideways just to cross between pads, so Side is now measured as staying turned
  the same way for an extended stretch while moving across panels: crossovers, chained drills, and stairs
  that carry across pads. Vertical drills, anchors and brackets don't count, and one-pad stairs count half.
  On Singles, Side means the feet are stacked on two vertical arrows for a large share of the chart.
- Jack, Jump, Stairs, Switch and Hi-Angle now also pick up charts where PIU Scores (Arrow Eclipse) shows the
  skill but ArcadeStat voters left it out.
- Added tags only fill gaps (community votes always win) and always count as a minor tag on the chart.
