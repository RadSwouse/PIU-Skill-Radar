How each level is calculated

  1. Chart level = the chart's level + its ArcadeStat tier vote (Top ≈ +1.25 … Bottom ≈ −0.25).
  2. Score adjustment = weighted around SS ±0 (SSS+ = +0.15 … A = −1.6).
  3. Side-tech discount: a chart counts lower for a tech it only has a little of. Value = level + adjustment − discount × (1 − tech share), where share is the tech's vote weight divided by the chart's top tag. The discount is 3 levels for common techs and smaller for rare ones (3 × tagged Lv18+ charts ÷ 50).
  4. Your level = weighted average of your best values filling 10 slots. A value above 22 fills extra slots (1 + every level above 22), so hard clears weigh more.
  5. A skill appears once you have 5 qualifying clears: charts where the tech is at least 25% of the top tag (any amount for rare techs or added tags).

Technique tags and tier votes come from the ArcadeStat community, with extra tags where voters left a tech out, from PIU Scores skill data and this site's own step-pattern analysis. Scores come from PIU Scores or your ArcadeStat export. Clears only; stage breaks don't count.
